The big idea you're testing. Forward pass: push inputs through to get outputs and the error. Backward pass: an output neuron's "blame" is δ = (o−t)·σ′. A hidden neuron's blame is the sum of downstream blames it fed into, each weighted by the connecting weight, times its own σ′ — that sum is the chain rule in action. Every weight's gradient is just δ_downstream × activation_upstream, and you step it down by η·gradient.
